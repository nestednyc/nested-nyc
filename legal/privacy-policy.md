# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nested ("**we**," "**us**") collects, uses, and shares information when you use Nested (the "**Service**"). It applies to both student accounts and organization accounts.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account and profile information.** When you sign up, we collect your email address (a university email for student accounts) and password (stored by our authentication provider, not by us in plain text). Your profile can include your name, username, school, major/class year, skills, bio, links, and a profile photo.

**Content you create.** Projects you post, community board posts and comments, likes and saves, event RSVPs and any answers you give to a host's questions, club membership applications and your answers to a club's questions, and connections you make with other students.

**Messages.** Direct messages you send and receive, and any files you attach to them. Message bodies are encrypted at rest. Some messages you receive may be introductory messages Nested's AI drafted on another student's behalf rather than something they typed themselves — see "Nested AI intros" below.

**Nested AI intros.** From time to time, we use profile information — including skills, school, and your existing connections — to identify students who might want to know each other, and have an AI system draft a short introductory message. That message is sent as a normal direct message from one student's account to the other's; the recipient's app marks it as AI-introduced. This runs in occasional batches we run by hand, not continuously or automatically.

**Photos and files.** Profile photos, project images, community post images, and message attachments you upload.

**Reports and communications.** If you report a post, comment, or profile, we collect what you submit. If you email us, we keep that correspondence.

**Automatically collected information.** We log IP addresses to enforce rate limits and prevent abuse. We use Vercel Web Analytics for aggregate usage data (like page views) — this doesn't use third-party advertising cookies or build ad-targeting profiles. Your signed-in session is kept in your browser's local storage so you don't have to log in on every visit.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Operate the Service — create and secure your account, show you projects, events, and other students, deliver your messages, and run the community board;
- Introduce students to each other — occasionally, we use profile and connection data to identify good matches and have an AI system draft an introductory direct message sent on a student's behalf;
- Send transactional email — join-request and connection notifications, new-message alerts, club/event updates, and (unless you opt out) a weekly digest;
- Prevent abuse — rate-limit requests, investigate reports, and enforce our Terms of Service;
- Improve the Service — understand aggregate usage patterns and fix problems.

We don't use your information to serve third-party advertising, and we don't sell your personal information.

## 3. How we share information

**With other users, by design.** Some information is public to anyone on the internet, signed in or not — including project listings, events, organization pages, and the name/username/photo/school of whoever posted them. Other information — your full profile, the community board, and the People directory — is visible only to signed-in students, and in some cases only to your connections or project teammates. Direct messages are visible only to you and the person you're messaging.

**With service providers**, who process information on our behalf and are bound to use it only to provide their service to us:

- **Supabase** — our database, authentication, and file-storage provider. Nearly everything described above is stored here.
- **Vercel** — our hosting provider, and the source of our aggregate usage analytics.
- **Resend** — our email-delivery provider, used to send the notifications described above.

**For legal reasons.** We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when we believe in good faith it's necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Nested, our users, or the public.

**Business transfers.** If Nested is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction; we'll notify you if your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 4. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service. If you delete a specific piece of content, it's removed going forward (residual copies may briefly remain in backups). If you delete your account (see below), we delete or de-identify your profile and content within a reasonable period, except where we need to retain something to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

## 5. Your choices

**Email preferences.** You can opt out of the weekly digest, or of non-essential email generally, from the unsubscribe link in any email. We may still send you emails that are necessary to operate your account (like security notices).

**Blocking.** You can block another user to stop them from messaging you.

**Access and correction.** You can view and edit most of your profile information directly in the app.

**Deletion.** Nested doesn't currently have a self-serve "delete my account" button. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at hi@nested.social and we'll process it within a reasonable period, subject to the retention exceptions above.

**Your state-law rights.** Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information — for example, the right to know what we've collected, request deletion, or opt out of certain kinds of sharing (California and a growing number of other states). We don't sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. To exercise any such right, contact us at hi@nested.social.

## 6. Security

We rely on database-level access controls (row-level security) so that, for example, other students can't read your private messages or gated profile fields directly, and message content is encrypted at rest. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's privacy

Nested is intended for university students and campus organizations, and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us at hi@nested.social and we'll delete it.

## 8. Where information is processed

We and our service providers are based in the United States, and information is generally processed and stored there. If you're accessing Nested from outside the United States, you're transferring your information there.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll post the updated policy with a new effective date and, where appropriate, notify you.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or to exercise any of the rights above: hi@nested.social.
